Track Pieces

Tracks interlock when connectors align. If rotation is wrong, placement auto-rotates to the nearest valid join. Fork switches are toggled by clicking their side lever.

Terrain

Stack blocks to raise terrain. Arch can be placed over straight, curved, or slope track, and blocks can stack above it to form tunnels. Ponds can be placed on open ground tiles. Canyon creates a 3x3 scene with an open center run and cactuses.

Trees

Use tree mode to place scenery on empty tiles.

Use animal mode to place roaming animals.
